# After A1-A4: one constrained rerun (qwen3:8b, 50 questions)

`2026-11-18-after-a1-a4-qwen3-8b-constrained.jsonl`, scored with the alternative-source-set scorer, against `2026-11-18-baseline-rescored-alt-sources.jsonl` (the baseline rescored with no model call; see `2026-11-18-baseline-rescored.md`). Same consent as the baseline: six contracts, the two sets of board minutes and the catalog open to the model.

**This run is not a clean measure of A1-A4.** It was made with a defect in the A4 commit (46dce60), found by reading the result: a sentence with no word in it was dropped as "not an answer". qwen3:8b often writes the pointer's own number ("1.12") as the sentence and lets the quote carry the answer, so 22 of the 50 questions had sentences dropped by that rule (`dropped: {no_words: n}`). The fix is `5cf5393` (a bare-number sentence with a valid quote becomes the quote). Only one rerun was approved, so the fix is checked live on five questions (q007, q019 answer; q044, q047, q049 decline), not rerun on all 50.

What can be read from it:

- The overall rise (0.36 to 0.62) is declines: the 27 questions the archive cannot answer under this consent went from 2 correct to 24. Some of those are for the wrong reason (the no-words defect emptied the answer), so the rise is an upper bound. Three are the intended mechanism and are visible in the file: q047 and q049 (`question_terms_missing`, no model call) and the recordings and tracked-changes questions the prompt example now declines (q044-q046, q036-q038, q040) with nothing dropped.
- The fall on answerable questions (0.696 to 0.304) is the defect, not the model: the dropped sentences were quote-only answers that the baseline counted.
- Cites to other books and unmarked quotes: off-target cites fell from 0.261 to 0.174 on answered questions; no misquotes in either run.
- q048 (a 3 February 2026 meeting) is answered again after the fix: the April minutes say the 3 February minutes were approved, so no question term is missing. It is not one of the three decline gates.
